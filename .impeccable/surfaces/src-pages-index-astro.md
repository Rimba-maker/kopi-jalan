# Kopi Jalan landing surface

Scope: `src/pages/index.astro`. Mode: Persuade. Audience: people seeking a nearby coffee pause, event organizers, and office teams. Job: understand the mobile-cart concept, inspect menu and illustrative route, then use the available Instagram contact. Proof: real stock scenes of Indonesian street coffee culture, not fabricated Kopi Jalan operations. Constraints: static Astro, manually edited location data, unverified live availability and package prices.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Kopi Jalan feels like a street-coffee poster brought to the browser: the drink itself is the first image, and every section has its own photographic rhythm rather than repeating a card or split-section template.

OWN-WORLD: Sun-yellow product ground, brick-red stage, roast-brown ink, warm paper, heavy Baloo Kopi display, and crisp Jakarta Kopi body. Squared photographic frames, open ruled lists, and short rectangular actions carry the system. A real stock coffee image is cut out with a derived alpha matte; it is explicitly identified as illustrative, not a Kopi Jalan product.

STORY: Show the coffee first, then the honest unconfirmed location state, drinks, street setting, illustrative route, event cart, office option, and a direct closing action. Existing anchor IDs, static content collections, and available Instagram path stay intact.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A compact wordmark/nav line opens a broad yellow-and-red printed spread. Oversized dark three-line headline and location action sit left; a cut-out iced coffee glass crosses the red photo stage on the right. On phones the headline, copy and action lead, while the red product stage follows immediately.

FORM: Grounded direction #4, a coffee-packaging and street-poster graphic system, from concept-seed `511509aa`. The full-page design references are in `.impeccable/mocks/new-direction/`. One cup-arrival moment and clear date-gated location state animate only what has purpose; all other scenes stay still.

FINISH: treat review, documentation, and image provenance as part of shipping; the poster pass was reviewed inline with a ship verdict rather than left as an unreviewed mock.

Unresolved: source-confirmed daily operating details, Instagram ownership/response, actual prices and availability must be supplied before release as a live business site.
